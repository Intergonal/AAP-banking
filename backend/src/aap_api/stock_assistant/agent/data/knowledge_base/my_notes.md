## Trading Psychology
The most important factor in investing is not strategy but psychology. Fear and greed drive markets more than fundamentals in the short term. Successful investors maintain discipline, stick to their plan, and avoid emotional decisions during market turbulence.

## Position Sizing
A general rule is to risk no more than 1-2% of your portfolio on any single position. This ensures that even a series of losses won't significantly damage your capital. Position size should be determined by both conviction level and portfolio risk tolerance.

## Recession Indicators
Key leading indicators of a recession include inverted yield curve (2yr > 10yr Treasury), declining consumer confidence, rising unemployment claims, and slowing manufacturing PMI. Historically, the yield curve inverts 12-18 months before a recession begins.
